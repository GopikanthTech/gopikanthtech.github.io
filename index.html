FAQ

Questions clients ask before we start.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. I work remotely with clients in any time zone, with weekly calls and regular written updates.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope. After a free 30-minute call I send a fixed quote with a timeline, so there are no surprises. Small pilots are a good way to start.

Will the AI agent sound like a bot?

No. I write it in your brand's tone and test it against real situations such as price objections, rude replies and language switching. When it isn't sure, it hands over to a person.

Is my customer data safe?

Agents only get the access you approve. I add data masking, read-only database access where it fits, approval steps for sensitive actions and audit logs. I can deploy in your own cloud account.

What happens after launch?

You get documentation and a handover. If you want ongoing improvements, monitoring and new features, a monthly retainer covers it.
