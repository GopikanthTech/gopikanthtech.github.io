FAQ

Questions clients ask before we start.

Something else on your mind? Ask on the free call or by email.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope. After a free 30-minute call I send a fixed written quote with the timeline and the expected monthly running cost of the AI, so there are no surprises.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Agents get read-only access where possible, sensitive data stays in your own cloud account, and I'm happy to sign an NDA before we look at anything.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. I work remotely with clients worldwide, and my working hours overlap with the UK and Europe, with calls arranged for US time zones.

What do I need to have ready?

Just the problem and access to the relevant documents or data. I'll help you work out the rest on the discovery call.

What happens after launch?

Every project includes 30 days of fixes. After that you can take over with the docs I hand you, or keep me on a monthly retainer for tuning, monitoring and new features.

Will the AI make things up?

I build agents to answer from your own documents and data, with guardrails and tests on real scenarios. When the agent isn't sure, it says so and hands over to a person.
